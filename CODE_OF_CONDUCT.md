# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome people of every background and level of experience, give and take feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use the site. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable, in the repository or anywhere you represent the project.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and every other project space. Many contributors are parishioners, priests and young people volunteering their time; treat them as you would in church.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
